What Spartan Phalanx is

The classical phalanx held because the line closed shoulder to shoulder, each shield covering the man beside it. A gap was a failure of the formation, not of one person. Spartan Phalanx applies that discipline to modern IT: operations, security operations, and the infrastructure those teams share.

We do not sell a single tool or a one-week audit theater. We stand in the line with your people — design the posture, test it, harden it, and prove it can take a hit.

Where we stand in the line

One practice covering the ground attackers and auditors both walk.

  • IT Operations

    Stable platforms, clear runbooks, change control that people actually follow, and monitoring that wakes the right person.

  • SecOps

    Detection, response, and the connective tissue between SOC, IT, and leadership — so alerts become decisions, not noise.

  • Security & architecture

    Identity, network, cloud, and endpoint posture designed as one shield wall — not a pile of overlapping products.

  • ISO & readiness

    ISO 27001 and related control work framed as operational truth: evidence you can find, control owners who know their job, gaps closed before the auditor arrives.

  • Penetration testing

    Adversary-minded tests against the paths that matter — then remediation partnership until the hole is closed and rechecked.

  • Infrastructure

    On-prem, hybrid, and cloud foundations that scale without becoming fragile: capacity, resilience, backup, and recovery you can trust.

Why the formation works

Efficacy is not a slide. It is overlapping coverage under real stress.

Overlap, not silos

Ops without SecOps leaves silent failures. SecOps without solid infrastructure chases ghosts. ISO work without both produces paperwork, not protection. Spartan Phalanx keeps those disciplines locked together so a finding in one column becomes a fix in the others.

Pressure before the breach

Penetration testing and tabletop drills press the line on purpose. You learn which shields slip, which signals are late, and which owners freeze — while you still control the clock.

Proof over promises

Readiness means evidence: configs, logs, tickets, restore tests, and retest results. We measure what held, what broke, and what changed — so boards and auditors see the same story your operators live.

Discipline that lasts

A phalanx is training, not a weekend surge. We leave playbooks, ownership maps, and habits your team can keep when the engagement ends — so the wall does not dissolve when the consultants leave.

How engagements run

  1. Scout the ground Current ops, SecOps, infra, and compliance posture — mapped without theater.
  2. Form the line Prioritized plan: harden, detect, document, and test in the order risk demands.
  3. Press and prove Implement, pen-test or assess, remediate, retest — until the formation holds.
  4. Hold the wall Retainers or follow-on cycles for continuous readiness as your stack and threats change.
